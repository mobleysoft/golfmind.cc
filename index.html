GolfMind

AI-assisted mental performance and focus training for golfers. This page runs two real, working pieces of that: a guided box-breathing timer (a real technique used by sports psychologists to lower pre-shot arousal) and a persistent pre-round mental-focus checklist.

What's real here, what isn't yet

The breathing timer and checklist above are real, functioning tools — the checklist state persists in your browser between visits. What's not built yet: any actual AI-personalized coaching, biometric input (heart-rate, HRV), or a tracked history of focus scores across rounds. This page will be extended honestly as those pieces become real, not described as if they already exist.
